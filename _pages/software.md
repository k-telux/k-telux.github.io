      <div class="om-section-heading"><h3>Choose the work, preserve its boundaries.</h3><p>Five kinds of request, each starting from what may and may not change.</p></div>
      <div class="om-tablist" role="tablist" aria-label="OpticalModeler work modes">
        <button type="button" role="tab" id="om-tab-design" aria-controls="om-panel-design">Design</button>

        <span class="om-tab-indicator" aria-hidden="true"></span>
      </div>
      <div class="om-panel" role="tabpanel" id="om-panel-design" aria-labelledby="om-tab-design"><p class="om-goal">Design a new measurement</p><p>An empty scene, a source-backed topology and permitted component assets.</p></div>
      <div class="om-panel" role="tabpanel" id="om-panel-reconstruct" aria-labelledby="om-tab-reconstruct"><p class="om-goal">Reconstruct a diagram or photographs</p><p>Authoritative inputs and an explicitly retained baseline.</p></div>
      <div class="om-panel" role="tabpanel" id="om-panel-correct" aria-labelledby="om-tab-correct"><p class="om-goal">Correct an accepted model</p><p>Frozen originals, protected families and declared movable degrees of freedom.</p></div>
      <div class="om-panel" role="tabpanel" id="om-panel-audit" aria-labelledby="om-tab-audit"><p class="om-goal">Audit an existing scene</p><p>Read-only evidence, actual saved geometry and precise findings.</p></div>
      <div class="om-panel" role="tabpanel" id="om-panel-present" aria-labelledby="om-tab-present"><p class="om-goal">Present the accepted result</p><p>A separate camera and light copy, with geometry and image provenance preserved.</p></div>
    </div>

    <div class="om-pillars">
      <article class="om-pillar" data-reveal><span class="om-pillar-index">01</span><h3>The optical path.</h3><p>Directed branches, actual working faces, apertures and detector endpoints. Free-space light and guided fiber keep distinct roles.</p></article>
      <article class="om-pillar" data-reveal data-delay="80"><span class="om-pillar-index">02</span><h3>The physical structure.</h3><p>Real mounting interfaces, table holes, fasteners and continuous supports, checked in the saved scene against their neighbors.</p></article>
      <article class="om-pillar" data-reveal data-delay="160"><span class="om-pillar-index">03</span><h3>The evidence.</h3><p>One scene lineage, fresh reopen measurements, actual image producers and a consistent manifest behind every claim.</p></article>
    </div>

    <div class="om-verdicts" data-reveal>
      <h3>Know what the result establishes.</h3>
      <dl>
        <div><dt><span class="om-chip om-pass">PASS</span></dt><dd>The declared applicable gates have current evidence.</dd></div>
        <div><dt><span class="om-chip om-partial">PARTIAL / SCOPED</span></dt><dd>A stated subset is checked; remaining blockers stay visible.</dd></div>
        <div><dt><span class="om-chip om-unverified">UNVERIFIED</span></dt><dd>Necessary evidence is missing or inconclusive.</dd></div>
        <div><dt><span class="om-chip om-blocked">BLOCKED</span></dt><dd>A known requirement fails.</dd></div>
      </dl>
      <p class="om-note">Independent community workflow, not affiliated with or endorsed by Thorlabs. A ready-for-review handoff does not certify installed hardware, alignment, optical performance or laser safety.</p>
